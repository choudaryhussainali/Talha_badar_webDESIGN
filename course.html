Qur'an & Tajweed

Read the Qur'an as it was revealed — correctly, confidently and with love. Nazira reading, the rules of Tajweed, memorisation plans and corrected recitation.

Enquire now How it works
Ideal forTeenagers and adults
FormatOne-to-one or small group · online
LevelAll levels — from complete beginners

The course

What it is

A course that takes you from your very first page of the Qur'an to fluent, correct recitation — and, if you wish, to memorisation.

Lessons are live, not recorded: the teacher hears you recite in every session and corrects you on the spot. Tajweed is taught as a practical skill, rule by rule, until it becomes the way you naturally read.

Modules: nazira reading · rules of Tajweed · memorisation plan · recitation correction
