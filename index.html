About Mufti Talha Badar

A scholar of the Qur'an, the law and the community

Mufti Talha Badar is an Islamic scholar, Mufti, researcher and educator based in Lahore, Pakistan. Since 2019 he has taught Qur'anic studies, Tajweed and Islamic jurisprudence online to learners in many countries — from teenagers to professionals and senior learners.

His work rests on three commitments: rigour in scholarship, sincerity in spirituality, and respect in dialogue.

  • Takhassus fil-Fiqh (Mufti course), Jamia Hujwaria, Lahore
  • M.Phil. Islamic Studies, The University of Lahore — 2026
  • Tajweed & Qira'at, graded Mumtaz — 2018
  • Author of research on the Ottoman Caliphate

M.Phil. conferred June 2026 · Ph.D. in progress, Superior University, Lahore.

The path

How to learn with him

A calm, four-step path from first contact to steady, measured progress.

01

Choose a course

Pick the course that fits your goal — Qur'an and Tajweed, Fiqh, Arabic, or Spirituality.

02

Intro call

A short conversation to settle your level, your timings and the right starting point.

03

Weekly online lessons

Live one-to-one or small-group lessons, with recitation heard and corrected every time.

04

Monthly progress review

Progress is reviewed each month, and the plan is adjusted honestly.
